The Problem

Exterior house painting is one of the most dangerous, labor-intensive, and weather-dependent trades in residential services. Despite technological advances in nearly every other home improvement category, painting remains stuck in the 1950s.

⚠️

Safety Hazard

Falls from ladders kill 300+ workers annually in the US. Workers' comp premiums for painters are among the highest in construction.

⏱️

Time Inefficiency

60% of job time is spent on setup, scaffolding, and repositioning—not actually painting. A 2,500 sqft house takes 3-4 days with a 2-person crew.

💸

Labor Shortage

Skilled painters are aging out. Average age is 42+, and young workers avoid the trade due to physical demands and low perceived prestige.

The Solution: Remote-Brain Architecture

Project Aero-Stroke abandons the traditional paradigm of stuffing expensive compute onto a flying platform. Instead, we deploy a "dumb muscle" hexacopter that carries only sensors and actuators, connected via fiber-optic tether to a ground-based AI supercomputer.

Key Technical Innovations

  • 🔗 Fiber-Optic Tether: 10Gbps, <2ms latency, immune to RF interference
  • 🧠 Ground Compute: RTX 4090 running Neural-Fly adaptive control
  • 📸 3D Gaussian Splatting: Pre-flight scan creates digital twin for training
  • 🎯 6ft Carbon Boom: Keeps drone in clean air while nozzle reaches wall
  • 🔋 LiPo Power: 25-min flight cycles, hot-swap batteries, no 400V death trap
